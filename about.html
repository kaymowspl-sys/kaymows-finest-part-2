About Us

Founded in 2023 in Pretoria, Kaymows Finest started with one bucket and a passion for cars.

Our mission is to give affordable luxury car care that protects your paint and the planet.

Our colours mean: Blue = trust, Light Blue = water, White = clean, Yellow = shine.
